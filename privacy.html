Privacy Policy

Is It a Cat? · Last updated 8 June 2026

Short version: Is It a Cat? processes your camera feed entirely on your own device. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, images, or video. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no servers receiving your data.

Camera access

The app asks for camera permission so it can show a live preview and detect whether the camera is pointed at a cat, a person, or neither. The detection model runs locally in the app using on-device computation. Camera frames are analyzed in real time and immediately discarded. They are never uploaded, never saved automatically, and never sent to any server or third party.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, or usage analytics. It has no login, no profile, and no advertising identifiers.

Saving and sharing images

If you tap "Save shot," the app creates an image of the current frame on your device and hands it to your device's normal save or share flow. This action is entirely user-initiated, the resulting file is stored only where you choose to put it, and the app does not retain a copy or transmit it anywhere.

Third-party services

The app loads its detection model (TensorFlow.js and a pre-trained object-detection model) from a public content delivery network when it first runs. This request retrieves software only; it does not send your images or any personal data. The app contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics SDKs.

Outbound links

The app may include a link to a third-party shopping page for cat toys. If you choose to tap it, you leave the app and that third party's own privacy policy applies. The app does not share any of your data with that destination.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
